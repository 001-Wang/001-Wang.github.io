Research Experience

Robot-Aligned 3D Gaussian Splatting for VLA Policy Adaptation
Jan. 2026 - Present
  • Built a real-to-sim-to-real data generation pipeline using 3D Gaussian Splatting to create robot-aligned wrist-camera demonstrations from real workspaces.
  • Fine-tuned VLA policies with generated image-action data for precise pre-contact reaching, reducing dependence on manual teleoperation.
  • Validated the system on a Franka Research 3 robot in a PC-disassembly benchmark with small targets such as screws, GPU, and RAM.
DA3-Initialized Semantic Gaussian Splatting for Scalable 3D Perception
Nov. 2025 - Mar. 2026
  • Built a DA3-initialized semantic Gaussian Splatting pipeline for scalable 3D scene representation from multi-view outdoor visual data.
  • Embedded persistent semantic labels into 3D Gaussian scenes for real-time rendering, semantic overlays, and autonomous scene understanding.
  • Optimized large-scale Gaussian primitives with sparse mask supervision, projected cross-attention, and cross-view consistency.
Uncertainty-Aware 3D Perception and Point Cloud Optimization
Sep. 2024 - Nov. 2025
  • Built an RFF-enhanced PointNet pipeline and proposed geometric uncertainty for outlier and structural-boundary detection.
  • Performed outlier removal, edge detection, and part segmentation within a single forward pass.
  • Applied Gaussian Processes, Bayesian optimization, and reinforcement learning for model optimization.

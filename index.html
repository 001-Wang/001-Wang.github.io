About

My goal is to build learning systems that connect strong visual and geometric perception with reliable robot action. I am especially interested in robot learning and VLA systems, 3D scene understanding and reconstruction, geometric representation learning, and methods that transfer from simulation or reconstructed environments to real robotic platforms.

I am interested in research and engineering opportunities in robotics, embodied AI, 3D vision, autonomous systems, and multimodal learning.

World Action Model / RVT-2 Future Ranker

Ongoing research · Robot learning / embodied AI

Exploring future-state reasoning for long-horizon manipulation by reranking RVT-2 action candidates with learned future representations and expert-alignment scoring in RLBench. The project studies how predicted future observations can improve action selection beyond a purely reactive manipulation policy.

RVT-2RLBenchTransformers Action RankingFuture Representation

PrecisionReach-GS

Submitted to IEEE Transactions on Robot Learning

A real-to-sim robot learning pipeline that combines calibrated wrist-camera perception, 3D Gaussian Splatting, and vision-language-action policies for precise robotic reaching and manipulation. The system connects photorealistic 3D reconstruction with real robot control and policy evaluation.

Robot Learning3D Gaussian Splatting VLAReal-to-SimRobot Calibration

GUARD

Submitted to Advanced Engineering Informatics

Geometric Uncertainty-Aware Robust Denoiser for point-cloud segmentation. GUARD combines learned geometric features with random Fourier feature Gaussian-process uncertainty to identify unreliable 3D measurements and perform denoising and segmentation in a single pass.

Point CloudsUncertainty Gaussian ProcessRFFPyTorch

Semantic Gaussian Splatting for 3D Damage Modeling

3D vision · multi-view reconstruction

A scalable multi-view framework that lifts sparse 2D supervision into persistent per-Gaussian semantic and damage attributes. The pipeline integrates modern 3D reconstruction with mask-guided optimization for consistent 3D localization across views.

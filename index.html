GateWAM: Bridging World Prediction and Robot Action

Jun. 2026 - Present · World-action models / embodied AI

Developed GateWAM, a confidence-gated world-action framework that combines historical keyframes, current observations, and predicted future stage goals for robotic manipulation. Built on memory-augmented BridgeVLA with a frozen PaliGemma backbone, its Multi-Future Prediction (MFP) module predicts compact latent hypotheses for multiple plausible goals. A reliability-aware gate uses prediction dispersion and policy signals to select between future-conditioned and goal-free actions.

Achieves 94.67% average success across 18 RLBench tasks, compared with 91.44% without future-goal conditioning. Evaluated on GemBench, COLOSSEUM, and three real-world manipulation tasks with a Franka Research 3 robot.

PrecisionReach-GS

Jan. 2026 - Present · arXiv:2610.35626 · 2026
Zuoxu Wang, Xiao Liang* · Texas A&M University
* Corresponding author

Reconstructs real workspaces with 3D Gaussian Splatting to generate robot-aligned wrist-camera observations and smooth reaching trajectories for VLA adaptation. With π0.5, 1,500 generated trajectories achieve 80.0% precise-reaching success across three motherboard targets, compared with 58.7% using 150 teleoperated demonstrations, with less than 10 minutes of active human capture.

DA3-Initialized Semantic Gaussian Splatting

Nov. 2025 - Mar. 2026 · 3D vision / scalable perception

Built a DA3-initialized semantic Gaussian Splatting pipeline for scalable 3D scene representation from multi-view outdoor visual data. The system embeds persistent semantic labels into 3D Gaussian scenes and uses sparse mask supervision, projected cross-attention, and cross-view consistency for scalable perception.

GUARD / Geometric Uncertainty-Aware Point Cloud Perception

Sep. 2024 - Nov. 2025 · arXiv:2610.09068 · 2026

Combines a multi-scale geometric transformer with a random Fourier feature Gaussian Process to filter unreliable points and segment parts in a single forward pass. On 2,745 real HDD point clouds, GUARD improves PointNet++ mIoU from 0.7739 to 0.8318; geometric uncertainty achieves 0.7931 corrupted-point detection F1 on annotated ScanNet samples.
